MISSION CONTROL

FLIGHT MANUAL

Your first expedition.

  1. Pick your hardware within 105 credits.
  2. Move with WASD / arrow keys or the on-screen arrows.
  3. Travel to all four city markers. Stop within a scan ring, then hold Space or SCAN to collect two packets per city. Each scan takes several seconds.
  4. Clouds, haze and dust can block optical scans. Save battery for the eclipse; wait in sunlight to recharge when needed.
  5. Reach the data relay station and hold E or TRANSMIT to send all eight packets. Transmission also takes time.

Complete the country mission within 300 game seconds. Location coordinates are approximate; the map, travel time, eclipses, weather and debris are educational gameplay, not live orbital or weather data.
