02 / Engineering review

What does balance mean?

The marker represents the mass-weighted center of your modules. In this game an off-center design has a small maneuvering penalty. Real control dynamics also depend on thruster geometry and inertia; this model is illustrative.

03 / TEST BENCH

Will it last through the night?

Run a three-minute power experiment. Change the imaging duty cycle and time in eclipse, then compare designs.

Each 60-second cycle starts in sunlight. Battery starts full. Values are scaled for education; propulsion and radio are off on this bench. Drafts assume an ideal connected power bus; disconnected builds cannot launch.
